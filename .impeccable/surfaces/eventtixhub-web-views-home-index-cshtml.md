## Direction contract

Scope: Афиш and event details (Persuade). Visitor browses on a phone for a specific evening across film, concerts and theatre in three cities.

THESIS: The Афиш is a poster wall, not a card grid. Every event is a typographic афиш in its art form's ink, with the date set on the poster. It refuses photo-thumbnail cards with a blue "buy" button.

OWN-WORLD: Newsprint paper, press ink, ultramarine for film, saffron for concerts, vermilion for theatre. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed 800 for posters and titles, Sofia Sans for reading. Square sheets, heavy ink rules, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees what is on across three art forms, narrows by type and city in one tap, picks a poster and reads its programme page, then chooses a date with "Избери места".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav; the masthead "АФИШ" in Display with one line naming the real cities; one row of type chips plus the city filter, other filters behind "Още филтри" on phones; the poster wall starting above the fold on 375 x 667, 2 across on phones, 4 on desktop. The primary action is the poster title link.

FORM: user-pinned афиш (no roll; seed key: none).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
